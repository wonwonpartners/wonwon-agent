Real-time intelligence for safety: detecting anomalies, preventing accidents, and staying ahead. Add WiralBot to your workforce. It not only detects anomalous conditions, but also gathers and analyzes real-time data to support accident prevention. A tailored expert team, ready to respond. By flexibly combining various sensors—such as RGB cameras, LWIR, LiDAR, microphones, and ultrasonic cameras—WiralBot adapts to each environment and determines anomalies in real time by analyzing fused data.
