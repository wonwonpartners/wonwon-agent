Hojun Choi, Co-founder & Co-CEO of LinqAlpha, joined UBS AIC 2025 in Hong Kong to speak about how AI agents are transforming financial research and industry operations. His talk focused on the diverging GenAI adoption patterns across financial institutions. Even within the same firm, analysts who can articulate precise instructions, set clear objectives, provide detailed examples, and critically evaluate AI output are pulling ahead in productivity and research impact. Hojun also shared how hedge funds, asset managers, and global banks are embedding AI into their workflows from firmwide training programs to bespoke use case development by business line.
