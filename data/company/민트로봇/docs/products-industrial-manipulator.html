The Pal-I Series is MINTROBOT’s first industrial robotic manipulator, developed as a core platform for high-performance automation. It supports software-definable development and is engineered for real-world deployment across a wide range of demanding industrial applications. 1. Precision Engineering, Unrivaled Accuracy All structural and positioning components of our manipulator are crafted using high-precision CNC machining. This ensures exceptional dimensional consistency, resulting in highly accurate motion trajectories and repeatable performance — critical for demanding tasks such as assembly, inspection, and micro-handling. 2. Joint Accuracy with ±10 Arcseconds Repeatability Each joint delivers uncompromising accuracy, achieving ±10 arcseconds of repeatability across all axes. This level of precision ensures stable, consistent operation — even in high-complexity or long-duration tasks where absolute repeatability is essential. 3. Proven Real-Time EtherCAT Control with Safety Our manipulator leverages a real-time EtherCAT communication backbone for ultra-low-latency motion control and seamless system integration. Every joint module is certified for functional safety, enabling safe operation in collaborative and safety-critical environments. It’s not just fast and precise — it’s industrially safe by design. Articulated Type General-Purpose Dexterity with Precision The articulated robot is your go-to solution for general-purpose automation. With its full 6 degrees of freedom, it excels at complex trajectories, orientation-sensitive tasks, and operations in dynamic or irregular environments. Precision-engineered to the core, each axis delivers ±10 arcseconds repeatability, enabling high-accuracy applications such as assembly, inspection, machine tending, and precision welding. From the delicate pick of a screw to the fluid sweep of a weld, this robot moves with the elegance of human intuition — yet never tires, never wavers, and never misses a mark.
