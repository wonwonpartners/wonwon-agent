Mintrobotis excited to announce the launch of its newly redesigned official website, marking a significant milestone in its global expansion and focus on cutting-edge software and AI solutions. The newly designed platform showcases the latest innovations in robotics while providing a more streamlined and user-friendly experience for our new and existing customers, partners, and stakeholders worldwide. The goal of the new website is to better reflect our evolving identity as a forward-thinking robotics company with an increased emphasis on software solutions. We've created a modern platform that allows easy access to all our products and services, including cutting-edge robotics technology, AI, and sustainable initiatives. Visitors can easily navigate key sections such as products, services, investor relations, and company news through an intuitive interface. What’s New on the Mintrobot Website? ‍ 1.       Multilingual Support As part of our global expansion efforts, the new website supports multiple languages, making it easier for international customers and partners to communicate with us. Whether you're in Asia, Europe, or the Americas, our website provides localized content for a seamless browsing experience. 2.      Modern Design & Enhanced Navigation The website has been completely revamped with a fresh, sleek design. The streamlined navigation allows visitors to quickly find detailed product specifications, software solutions, or the latest company news. Additionally, the site is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices, ensuring a consistent user experience across all platforms. 3.     Shift Towards Software Focus With increasing demand for software-based robotic solutions, we have shifted our focus to emphasize AI and software products. This change reflects our effort to integrate intelligent software solutions that enhance automation and efficiency across industries, alongside our top-tier robotic hardware. 4.     Sustainability & ESG Our commitment to sustainability and ethical business practices is highlighted on the new website. You can explore our ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance)initiatives, which aim to reduce our carbon footprint, promote inclusive hiring practices, and introduce sustainable manufacturing processes. 5.     Comprehensive Product & Service Information The upgraded product pages offer detailed specifications, technical datasheets, customer case studies, and insights into our ongoing research. Customers can gain deeper insights into our latest research and development activities. 6.     Online Purchase System The website now features a system for directly ordering and purchasing robotic products. This crucial change ensures pricing transparency and simplifies the purchasing process for customers, enabling them to buy products easily without confusion from distributor pricing differences. 7.      Investor and Recruitment Information A newly introduced investor section provides shareholders and potential investors with the latest financial reports, business forecasts, and corporate governance information. It offers important insights into Mintrobot’s business operations with a focus on transparency. Additionally, a new recruitment page has been added to attract top talent. A Step Towards the Future With this website revamp, Mintrobot aims to strengthen its presence in the global robotics market and continue leading the way toward innovative software solutions and a sustainable future. We hope that our customers and partners will have a better experience with the new website and look forward to continued growth and development.
