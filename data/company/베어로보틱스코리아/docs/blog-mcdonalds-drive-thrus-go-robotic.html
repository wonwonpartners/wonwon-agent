Faced with labor shortages, rising costs, and the explosion of delivery apps, they've turned to robots like Servi for help. Late-Night Hustle in Dallas Imagine a bustling McDonald's in Dallas, Texas. It's late at night, and the orders are flying in – from in-store customers, the drive-thru, and a flood of delivery apps. The overnight managers, Dalia Gaona and Bernice Alvarado, are constantly on the move. "We get a lot of DoorDash and Uber Eats coming at night now," says Gaona. "It's a huge part of our business." To keep up with the delivery demand, the restaurant installed a special window for drivers to pick up orders quickly. But there was a catch: the window was far from the kitchen, making it a hassle for the already busy staff. Servi to the Rescue Enter Servi, the delivery robot. Since 2022, Servi has been shuttling orders from the counter to the delivery window, allowing the staff to focus on customers in the store and drive-thru. "It's a lifesaver when we're short-staffed," Alvarado explains. "Servi helps us handle the volume of orders and keeps things running smoothly." The results are impressive: Servi has made over 10,000 deliveries, covering 121 miles and carrying more than 57,000 pounds of food. It's not just about convenience – Servi has helped the McDonald's team operate more efficiently with fewer employees, saving on labor costs. Gaona's advice for other restaurant owners considering robot servers? "Choose a model that can handle your service volume. We're a busy store, so we need a larger model like Servi Plus to carry multiple orders at once." Her recommendation is exactly why Bear Robotics offers a complimentary consultation to assess your business needs and identify opportunities for enhancing operational efficiency with our service robots.
