The two companies have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), making LG Energy Solution the exclusive supplier of cylindrical battery cells for Bear Robotics’ flagship service robots, including the Servi Plus and Carti 100 . The partnership will kick off next year and promises not just battery supply but also collaboration on new technologies. Why This Partnership Matters Bear Robotics is a global leader in AI-powered autonomous service robots, operating in over 20 countries. These robots, used in restaurants, hotels, warehouses, and other public spaces, require reliable and safe energy to function smoothly in high-traffic environments. LG Energy Solution is stepping in to provide cutting-edge battery technology designed for safety and durability. LG’s advanced materials—such as high-quality NCMA cathodes and reinforced ceramic-coated separators—ensure its batteries meet the strict safety standards required for robotics. This makes them a perfect match for Bear Robotics, whose products interact directly with people and play critical roles in automation. Innovation and Growth in the Robotics Market The partnership also opens doors to innovation. LG Energy Solution and Bear Robotics will work together to develop new technologies, strengthening their position in the growing robotics industry. With the global robotics market booming, this collaboration is a major step toward smarter, safer, and more efficient automation solutions. For LG Energy Solution, the move aligns with its broader strategy to expand beyond electric vehicles into emerging markets like robotics, urban air mobility (UAM), and maritime vessels. As outlined in its vision, “Empower Every Possibility,” LG aims to diversify its business portfolio into high-growth, future-focused industries. For Bear Robotics, the partnership brings enhanced capabilities to their products, allowing them to continue redefining automation in public and industrial spaces. What’s Next? This collaboration represents a major milestone for both companies and for the robotics industry as a whole. By combining LG Energy Solution’s expertise in safe and reliable energy with Bear Robotics’ leadership in AI-powered robotics, the two companies are paving the way for new advancements that will shape the future of automation. As this partnership grows, we can expect to see even more exciting developments from these two innovators. Together, they’re setting a new standard for the robotics industry—and the journey is just beginning.
