At Bear Robotics, our robotic service solution Servi has been a saving grace for businesses who might need a little extra help with bussing tables, running orders, hosting, and more. In the last few years, we’ve seen incredible growth as more restaurants, hotels, senior living communities, arenas, and entertainment venues adopt our automation solutions. In fact, we have scaled from a scrappy start-up to a renowned service solutions provider with robots in 42 states and counting! In our mission to elevate the hospitality industry, we’ve set our sights on deploying a Servi in every state across the U.S. Therefore, we’re super excited to announce we just broke new ground with Servi robots in both Alaska and Hawaii. Automating Alaska Through our partnership with NANA Management Services (a subsidiary of Sodexo), we were able to introduce Alaska to hospitality automation as Servi became the state’s first-ever service robot. In all, Servi made landfall across four senior living communities including Anchorage Pioneer Home, Ketchikan Pioneer Home, Marlow Manor, and Fairbanks Pioneer Home. Servi is an excellent addition to senior living communities as it allows both residents and staff to build better connections through more meaningful mealtimes. Since Servi is able to handle all the back and forth of bussing dishes and running food, staff are freed to lend more attention to their residents. Aside from elevating senior living communities, Servi is also now in attendance at Alaska Pacific University. While schooling students and faculty alike on superior service, Servi will be eliminating repetitive work for staff by bussing tables in the dining hall. Enhancing Hawaiian Hospitality As one of the very first service robots in Hawaii and the first ever Bear Robotics tech in Honolulu, Servi is now an integral part of service at Kalakaua Gardens, one of the island’s premier senior living communities. Kalakaua Gardens just deployed two Servi robots to help with food service operations. Like many senior living communities around the country, Kalakaua Gardens has felt the sting of staffing shortages. By implementing automation, their team members are able to easily connect with residents to provide elevated dining experiences. What’s Next Now that Servi has a presence across our 49th and 50th states, there’s no limit to how far this technology will take us.
