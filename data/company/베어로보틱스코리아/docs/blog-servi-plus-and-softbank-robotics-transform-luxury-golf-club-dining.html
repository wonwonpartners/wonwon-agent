Following a triumphant deployment trial at the prestigious Maria Golf Links by Pacific Golf Management Co. in Kisarazu City, Chiba Prefecture, Servi Plus left a lasting impression. Yasuhisa Kanetsugu, Sales Promotion Department Manager at PGM, couldn't contain his excitement as he praised Servi Plus for its modern design and ability to flawlessly deliver meals, even on challenging, sloped terrains and various floor types. 100 units of Servi Plus are now set to revolutionize the dining experience at multiple PGM clubs! Kanetsugu didn't hold back in expressing how seamlessly Servi Plus integrated into their club, instantly becoming an indispensable part of their staff. The robot’s ability to carry multiple entrees is perfect for typical groups of four at golf courses, making dining service efficient and elevated. Par Excellence: Advanced Features in Robotic Service Seamless Luxury Integration Designed to effortlessly blend into upscale settings, Servi Plus enhances operational efficiency and elevates service standards by adapting to detailed customer settings. Automated Precision and Visual Appeal Servi Plus self-drives to its destination, creating a captivating dining experience with a responsive lighting function that enhances dish presentation. Flexible Travel and Multi-Table Excellence Servi Plus excels in detailed travel settings, enabling changes in direction and speed. Its LED messaging facilitates 360-degree dish identification, making it a master at serving multiple tables simultaneously. Capacity Powerhouse With a robust 88 lb max capacity and a configurable four-tier tray system, Servi Plus streamlines the delivery of over 16 dishes at once, contributing to enhanced operational efficiency. Agile Navigation in Narrow Spaces Defying its large capacity, Servi Plus navigates effortlessly through narrow spaces with a minimum width of 65 cm, showcasing a small turning radius and industry-leading navigation for flexibility in confined areas. Obstacle Avoidance and Master Stability Equipped with LiDAR and industry-leading suspension, Servi Plus ensures stability while handling drinks and soups, and safe navigation by precisely detecting and avoiding obstacles in various environments. Servi Plus aims to improve operational efficiency, allowing staff to focus more on customers and enhancing overall customer service and performance.
