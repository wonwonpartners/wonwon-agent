Attendees are invited to visit Booth #6217 in the North Hall to see how Bear Robotics is reshaping restaurant operations through intelligent automation. With rising labor costs and growing service demands, operators are turning to automation that delivers real-world results. Bear Robotics will present live demonstrations of its flagship products - including Servi , Servi Plus , and Carti 100 - highlighting multi-robot workflows, safety, and efficiency in a dynamic restaurant setting. Servi Plus is an award-winning server assistant designed for dependable food running and table bussing. Servi offers a compact, flexible solution ideal for hybrid workflows in modern dining environments. Carti 100 is a high-capacity, configurable logistics robot engineered to support back-of-house operations with up to 100 kg of payload. “We’re excited to show how automation can work side-by-side with staff to enhance service and improve operational efficiency,” said John Ha, CEO of Bear Robotics. “These technologies are not future concepts—they’re already delivering results in restaurants around the world.” Giveaway: Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer AI Glasses Attendees can enter a special raffle by texting BEAR to 650-240-1881 . One winner will receive a pair of Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer AI glasses with Transitions® lenses. Winner must be present at Booth #6217 on May 19 to claim the prize. Full terms available online .
