With multiple upgrades, iterations, and advances, Bear Robotics has been able to shape the world of workflow automation with robots suited to just about any environment. However, we’ve never stopped striving to get bigger and better, and that’s where Servi Plus comes in. Servi Plus is a heavy-duty take on our original Servi robot. This high-capacity automation tool features an incredible 88 lb payload and 16+ dish capacity for businesses that need extra oomph when it comes to running food, delivering drinks, or bussing tables. Whether you’re streamlining service at a large buffet or you simply have an incredibly busy restaurant, hotel, or senior living community, Servi Plus is the perfect choice for those looking to increase their service capacity. New Tech, New Territory Of course, this robot includes all the cutting-edge technology and autonomous navigation advances our customers have come to expect from Servi. LiDar sensors and multiple cameras allow Servi Plus to navigate any space with poise and precision while incredible software innovations such as Multi-Robot Mode and Auto Return make this workflow solution as smart as it is supportive. However, Servi Plus does come equipped with quite a bit of new features we haven’t released on any other models to date. These upgrades include Multi-Destination capability, driving optimization for smoothly navigating ramps and delivering liquids, as well as a robust suspension system designed after the one used in the Mars Rover by NASA! Servi Plus also comes with some incredibly cool responsive LED lighting which includes a dot matrix display for custom messaging, food highlight lighting for visual appeal, and perimeter lighting for greater visibility and user communication. Bigger. Better. Bear. Of course, the biggest new feature is that it’s well… big. Capable of carrying extra-large trays and bus tubs, with custom tray configurations, Servi Plus was designed to add more automated muscle to any business. We believe these features alone make it a game-changer for any business that simply needs more help and a higher-capacity service solution. Because when servers are able to stay on the floor and assist your guests, the real benefits of robotics can be seen through higher ratings, bigger tips, and happier customers.
