On January 17, 2025, LG CNS and Bear Robotics signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to co-develop cutting-edge AMRs and integrated robot control solutions. This collaboration aims to revolutionize logistics operations with seamless automation and remote robot management systems. Innovating Logistics with Advanced AMRs Headquartered in Silicon Valley, Bear Robotics is a leader in AI-driven service robots, currently operating over 20,000 robots across 20 countries. With proprietary fleet management technology, Bear Robotics can coordinate up to 1,000 robots in a single location efficiently. This expertise will now contribute to LG CNS's efforts to create AMRs tailored for various industrial applications, from heavy-duty transport in large facilities to smaller robots optimized for automated logistics. The partnership will result in solutions that adapt to the unique requirements of logistics centers, reducing operational costs and significantly improving efficiency. Key developments include: Remote robot operation Path optimization and traffic management Real-time performance monitoring Centralized control systems A Shared Vision for Smarter Logistics LG CNS brings extensive experience in smart logistics, offering solutions such as unmanned guided vehicles (AGVs), AI-powered picking robots, and advanced AI vision systems. Combining these capabilities with Bear Robotics' fleet management and AI expertise, the partnership will enable high-efficiency automated solutions designed to transform the logistics sector. Junho Lee, Senior Vice President of LG CNS Smart Logistics & City Division, highlighted the potential impact of the collaboration: "By integrating Bear Robotics' exceptional AMR and control solutions with LG CNS's smart logistics expertise, we aim to lead the digital transformation of the logistics industry. Our joint efforts will enhance competitiveness and cost efficiency across logistics operations." Transforming Logistics for the Future The collaboration will also leverage LG CNS’s proprietary mathematical optimization and digital twin technologies to maximize the performance of the jointly developed AMRs. These innovations will ensure seamless scalability, enabling logistics centers to meet dynamic industry demands. As logistics becomes an increasingly critical part of modern business, the LG CNS and Bear Robotics partnership is poised to set a new standard for efficiency and reliability. Together, they aim to deliver a robust, automated logistics ecosystem tailored to the evolving needs of industries worldwide. This collaboration underscores Bear Robotics’ commitment to empowering industries with innovative robotic solutions that not only enhance operational efficiency but also redefine the future of logistics. Stay tuned for updates as this groundbreaking partnership unfolds.
