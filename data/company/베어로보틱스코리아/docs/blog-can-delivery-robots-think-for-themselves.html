Sep 3 Written By Alison Suzuki A good plan is key to success. But things don’t always go according to plan. When they don’t, we improvise. That’s what makes us human. But what about robots? Not all robots are created equal. That’s why we’ve engineered ours to adapt to the unexpected, knowing that how delivery robots react to change will be the difference between a logistics setback and a successful delivery, a bad review and a return customer. Introducing Smart Parking. What is Smart Parking and why does it matter? Imagine a server usually stands at the head of a table, but this time there’s a baby stroller in that spot. They’ll look for the next best place to interact with customers, never missing a beat. Smart Parking brings this same approach to Bear Robotics delivery robots. Whether patrons shift their table, add a chair, or stack birthday presents in the designated delivery spot, our autonomous delivery robots seamlessly adjust to keep the food fresh and on time, every time. The same applies across any number of industries, from hospitality and assisted living, to shipping centers and office space. This intelligent design is key to running an efficient business where customers are satisfied, especially for busy restaurants where: Staff may be limited: The robot can increase delivery speed, bringing multiple deliveries at once. Areas are dynamic: Chairs and tables might be frequently moved, potentially blocking the original destination. How Smart Parking works While the technology behind Smart Parking is equally complex and advanced, using it couldn’t be easier. Simply choose the preferred delivery point, then pick an area around that point to be the back-up option, or plan B. You can think of it like a virtual circle. Anytime the preferred serving spot isn’t available, your delivery robot will pause to see if the spot becomes available, and if not, will quickly pick the next best spot to make the delivery. Smart Parking equals real ROI The businesses we work with tell us how much their customers love being served by our delivery robots. But the benefits extend far beyond the novelty and excitement people experience, right down to dollars in the bank. This happen in multiple ways, including: More successful deliveries : and that means more people served, faster. Streamlined operations : Your staff can focus more on customer needs and personal connection, and less time running orders. Happier customers : People love tech when it works well. Smart Parking ensures the tech delivers a great experience, and happy customers are repeat customers. Real-world results Since launching Smart Parking, we've seen numerous successes at locations where blocked delivery spots were a common occurrence. The results speak for themselves: increased delivery success rates, reduced staff intervention, and ultimately, a boost in overall operational efficiency. Smart Parking is currently being used across industries and with name brands you know and trust, names like McDonald's, United Airlines, Marriott, and more. How Smart Parking compares to other delivery robots Instead of checking the box on making our delivery robots respond to the unexpected, we’ve worked to make it the most adaptive, and easy-to-use feature on the market. This means we’ve defined what it means to serve businesses and their customers in three key ways: Unmatched flexibility : Smart Parking has the widest customizable parking radius of any delivery robot on the market, up to four times larger than leading competitors. On-robot tuning : There’s no complicated back-end programming. Fine-tune your parking radius directly on the robot’s screen with an easy-to-use interface. Continual improvement : Smart Parking is just the latest in a series of continual improvements. Our customers are always helping us make our service robots work better. Frequently asked questions Q.
