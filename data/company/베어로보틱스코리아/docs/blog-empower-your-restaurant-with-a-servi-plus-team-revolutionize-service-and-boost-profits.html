Imagine a flawlessly running restaurant where a fleet of intelligent Servi Plus robots efficiently handles repetitive day-to-day tasks. With multiple robots, you can send more orders simultaneously without waiting for one to return. This allows for dedicated robots for food running and others for bussing, eliminating the need to switch roles and enhancing overall efficiency. Your paid staff is now free to focus on customers and what they do best - whether it be creating a personalized and valuable guest experience or making sure food items are prepared and presented with the highest standards, Servi Plus will help you get there. With a lower cost to worker ratio and higher rates of efficiency and productivity, Servi Plus robots will not only improve your bottom line, they provide the customer with a new, unique dining experience enjoyed by all ages. The Servi Plus Advantage: Streamlined Efficiency and Proven Results Powered by Servi Plus, our multi-robot system is a well-coordinated team of self-driving assistants designed to optimize your restaurant's operations. Multiple Servi Plus robots collaborate seamlessly, using advanced AI to navigate your space and adapt to the unique flow of your restaurant's activities. Bear Robotics’ multi-robot systems are the best on the market, offering unparalleled performance and adaptability. Each Servi Plus robot operates independently with its own sensors, ensuring they seamlessly adjust to your restaurant’s changing needs. These robots exhibit human-like intelligence, making them exceptionally capable in various tasks. One of their key abilities is queuing , where robots line up and move forward as the first one leaves, just like people waiting in line. This ensures a smooth flow of operations without any hitches. Additionally, they excel at yielding , meaning they can negotiate deadlock situations by waiting for one robot to pass through a narrow way first. This ability to yield keeps the workflow uninterrupted and efficient. Furthermore, our robots are adept at finding the fastest route to their destination. Unlike systems that follow a fixed path, our robots navigate by the quickest route possible, thanks to their sophisticated mapping capabilities. This feature maximizes efficiency and reduces delivery times, enhancing the overall dining experience for your customers. Together, these capabilities make our Servi Plus robots invaluable assets in daily operations. Our fully autonomous multi-robot mode is customized to your restaurant's layout, ensuring smooth and efficient service at all times.
