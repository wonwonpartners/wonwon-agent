What if you could turn those articles into spoken-word audio or video explainers without recording anything yourself? With AI Studios , you can. Using tools like Article to Video and URL to Video , AI Studios lets you convert blog content into narrated media in just a few minutes. Whether you want a full video with an on-screen presenter or just an audio version of your blog, the platform gives you everything you need in one place. Here’s how to do it in five easy steps. ‍ ‍ Step 1: Paste Your Blog or Insert the URL Start by choosing how to bring your blog into the platform. If it’s already live, use the URL to Video tool to paste the link. If it’s still in draft form or unpublished, paste the content into Article to Video . AI Studios will automatically scan the blog and pull out the key information. It then generates a script based on the content, giving you a strong starting point for audio or video. Step 2: Choose Your Format — Audio or Video Once the script is generated, you can choose how you want your blog presented. You have a few options: Create a full video with an AI avatar Export the script as audio only Use a natural-sounding AI voice Clone your own voice if you want it to sound like you Read the blog exactly as written with minimal AI rephrasing You don’t need to build a video unless you want one. Audio-only is perfect for turning blog posts into podcast-like content or accessibility-friendly formats. Step 3: Customize the Voice, Style, and Visuals If you choose to create a video, you’ll have full control over how it looks and sounds. You can: Pick from over 150 avatars Select a voice and language from 80+ options Choose a video template that fits your brand Upload your own images or video clips Let AI Studios find visuals from stock libraries or generate them using AI Set the tone, pacing, and video length This step helps you tailor the final result to your audience, brand, and platform. Step 4: Preview and Make Adjustments Once your video or audio is generated, you’ll be able to review it right inside the platform. You can make quick edits to: Update the script Switch out visuals Change the avatar or voice Adjust timing, emphasis, or layout Translate the video into any of 80+ supported languages Everything is editable, and there’s no need to open any external software. Step 5: Export and Repurpose When your content is ready, you can export it in your preferred format. Share it as: A full video for your website, YouTube channel, or social media An audio version for use in podcasts, blog embeds, or accessibility tools Short clips using Video Highlights , which automatically identifies and trims the most engaging parts of your video into social-ready formats From a single blog post, you can now create video, audio, and short-form content—without extra production time. Final Thoughts AI Studios makes it easy to turn a blog post into a fully voiced, shareable format. Whether you're aiming to increase accessibility, boost engagement, or repurpose written content for new platforms, tools like Article to Video and URL to Video give you everything you need in one workflow. You don’t have to record your own voice. You don’t need a studio or editing software. And you don’t need to create new content from scratch. Just bring your blog—and let AI Studios do the rest.
