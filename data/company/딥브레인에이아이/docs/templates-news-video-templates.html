What is an AI video template for AI News? An AI video template for AI News is a ready-made format that helps creators and organizations produce professional news videos quickly with the help of AI tools. These templates are designed for newscast-style content, announcements, and updates, and often feature layouts similar to broadcast news shows. They work with AI avatars, text-to-speech, and auto-subtitle generation, allowing users to turn scripts or bullet points into polished AI News videos. You can also use AI avatars as AI anchors, delivering the news naturally and professionally without the need for filming or voice recording. ‍ How do I use an AI Studios video template to create news videos? To create a news video in AI Studios, start by choosing a news video template or breaking news video template from the Templates page.
